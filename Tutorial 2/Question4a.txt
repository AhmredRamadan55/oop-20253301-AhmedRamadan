Lab Sheet 2 — Tutorial, Question 4(a)

Error: The break statement is missing after case 1. Without it, when num is 1 the program prints both "One" and "Three".

Corrected switch:
switch (num) {
    case 1:
        System.out.println("One");
        break;
    case 3:
        System.out.println("Three");
        break;
}
