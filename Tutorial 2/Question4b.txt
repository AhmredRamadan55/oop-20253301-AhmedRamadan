Lab Sheet 2 — Tutorial, Question 4(b)

Error: The break statement is missing after case 'u'. Without it, execution falls through to default and prints "Not vowel" as well. Add break before default.

Corrected switch:
switch (ch) {
    case 'a':
        System.out.println("Vowel");
        break;
    case 'e':
        System.out.println("Vowel");
        break;
    case 'i':
    case 'o':
    case 'u':
        System.out.println("Vowel");
        break;
    default:
        System.out.println("Not vowel");
}
